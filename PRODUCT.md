# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Language learners who want to read authentic foreign language materials (lyrics, articles, news) but struggle with unfamiliar vocabulary and pronunciation. They need a distraction-free, highly readable environment to focus on text comprehension.

## Product Purpose
SLM (SS4 Language Model / Immersive Reader) turns any raw text into a living, interactive document by highlighting mapped vocabulary terms and showing inline translations/pronunciation key-value data on demand. Success is defined by high text comprehension, minimal user interaction friction, and an aesthetic that supports long reading sessions.

## Brand Personality
*   **Focus-First**: Calm, minimalist, and utilitarian.
*   **Sophisticated**: Editorial, polished, and premium.
*   **Clean**: Restrained, structured, and intentional.

## Anti-references
*   Childish, cartoonish, or gamified language platforms (e.g., Duolingo).
*   Standard Material Design 3 blueprints (which feel like generic developer layouts).
*   High-density SaaS dashboards with excessive side navigation and status charts.

## Design Principles
1.  **Text is the Hero**: The user's reading passage takes center stage. Controls, stats, and sidebars are visually secondary and easily hidden.
2.  **Zen-like Calm**: Minimize animations to only subtle, high-performance transitions. Use a harmonious, low-contrast neutral palette to prevent eye strain.
3.  **Context preservation**: Translations and annotations must never obscure the reading flow or cause page layouts to shift.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
*   Strict WCAG AA/AAA contrast ratios for text on backgrounds.
*   Accessible keyboard navigation support (ESC to close popovers).
*   Full support for `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` layout adjustments.
